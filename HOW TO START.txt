GALLIUM - how to start it

1. Install Node.js if you don't have it: https://nodejs.org (click the big LTS button, then run the installer).
   You only do this once.

2. Double-click the start file for your computer:
     Mac:     Start Gallium (Mac).command
              (If your Mac says it's from an unidentified developer, right-click it, choose Open, then Open again.)
     Windows: Start Gallium (Windows).bat
              (If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.)

3. Gallium opens in your browser. Sign in as  demo  /  gallium123

Keep the black window open while you use Gallium. Close it to stop.

To try it on your phone: keep it running on your computer, then on your phone (same Wi-Fi)
open  http://YOUR-COMPUTER'S-IP:3000  and choose "Add to Home Screen".
